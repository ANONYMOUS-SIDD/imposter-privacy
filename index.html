01

Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Imposter handles user information. Imposter is a pass-and-play party word game in which one or more players are secretly assigned the role of imposter and do not know the round's secret word. The App is distributed through the Google Play Store.

By installing or using Imposter, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. This Policy applies solely to the App and does not extend to any third-party services, websites, or applications that may be referenced within it.

Summary: Imposter does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All data you enter remains exclusively on your device.
02

Information We Do Not Collect

Imposter has been built to avoid the collection of personal data entirely. Specifically, the App does not collect, request, or process:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact information.
  • User accounts, credentials, or authentication data of any kind.
  • Analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics.
  • Advertising identifiers or advertising-related data.
  • Location data, whether precise or approximate.
  • Access to contacts, calendars, camera, microphone, or files on your device.
  • Crash reports or diagnostic information transmitted to external servers.
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or network information.
03

Information Stored Locally

Imposter stores the following information exclusively on your device, using the platform's private application storage. This information is never transmitted to any server, third party, or external service:

  • Player names entered by you during a round.
  • Custom words created by you through the in-app editor.
  • Word categories selected in the Game Setup screen.
  • Word pick history, used solely to reduce the repetition of recently used words across rounds.
  • Language preference for the in-game word display (English, Nepali, or both), kept separate from the App's interface language.
  • App preferences including sound effects, volume, and haptic feedback settings.

You retain complete control over this data. It is deleted automatically upon uninstallation of Imposter, or manually through the in-app options.

04

System Permissions

Imposter does not request or require any Android runtime permissions. The App functions fully offline and does not access any device features that would grant access to personal information. Specifically, Imposter does not request:

  • Camera or microphone access.
  • Location services of any kind.
  • Contacts, calendar, or call log access.
  • Photos, media, or external file storage.
  • Phone state, device identifiers, or network information.
  • Internet or network access.
Note: Standard system events such as haptic feedback and local audio playback are used during gameplay but do not require or grant any privacy-sensitive permission on Android.
05

Third-Party Services

Imposter does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics providers, advertising networks, or cloud storage services. The App operates as a self-contained program and does not establish network connections to external services for the purpose of data collection or transmission. Imposter is fully functional in airplane mode and requires no internet connection at any point.

06

Children's Privacy

Imposter does not knowingly collect personal information from any individual, including children under the age of 13 or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction. Since the App does not collect personal information of any kind, it is suitable for users of all ages without restriction.

07

Data Security

All data stored by Imposter is contained within your device's private application storage sandbox, protected by the operating system's native security mechanisms. Neither the developer nor any third party has access to this data.
